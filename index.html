What is the Dunning-Kruger effect?

It's a cognitive bias where people with limited knowledge or skill in a subject overestimate their own competence — because the same lack of skill that produces poor performance also stops them from recognizing it. As real understanding grows, confidence often dips before eventually catching back up to match actual ability.
